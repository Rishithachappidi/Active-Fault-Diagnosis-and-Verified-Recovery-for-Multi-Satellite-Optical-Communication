# Limitations and research boundaries

- **Independent baseline validation pending:** source/SLX parity was checked statically; MATLAB and Simulink were unavailable. The new port fixture detects regression, not fidelity to independent ground truth. The original repository's Eq.26 factor differs from the PDF.
- **Native extension unexecuted:** MATLAB files and a separate Simulink builder are supplied but have not been compiled or run here. The native observer/scheduler follows the same design; MATLAB random streams and loss draw timing differ from JavaScript, so stochastic sample-path parity is not claimed. Native campaigns/optimization are not duplicated; the shared Node/browser implementation owns those engines.
- **No flight claim:** no HIL/SIL qualification, processor measurements, certification, fault containment proof, or flight readiness. Resource profiles are hypothetical operation budgets and a nominal 32 KiB state estimate.
- **Local attitude dynamics:** no orbit/translation propagation, rigid-body flexibility, wheels, magnetorquers, rate-limited torques, sensor bias, eclipse, solar pressure fidelity or thermal power model. Visual formation positions are schematic; trails trace body-axis directions rather than position trajectories.
- **No global MRP treatment:** no shadow switching; component-difference errors are not SO(3) geodesic errors. Divergence/large-chart excursions are failed runs.
- **Empirical adaptive law:** no Lyapunov or stability proof for threshold scaling, compensation, hard budgets, packet losses or zero-effectiveness cases. Full axis loss violates the paper's positive-effectiveness assumptions. Success thresholds are mission-specific assumptions.
- **Diagnosis ambiguity:** torque residuals confound effectiveness, inertia, disturbance and noise. Excitation gates and uncertainty scores are transparent but not statistically calibrated. No confusion matrix or detection-delay distribution is claimed. Confidence is called evidence in the UI to avoid probabilistic overstatement.
- **Communication abstraction:** one sender broadcast succeeds/fails for all receiving neighbors, no queue, delays, collision, retransmission, timestamp drift or independent link failures. Initial neighbor states are pre-shared.
- **Finite-horizon metrics:** terminal settling and recovery can change if the mission is extended. Null recovery is censored; intermittent loss has ambiguous onset-relative recovery.
- **Small exploratory optimizer:** 48 candidates, two training environments; no global optimality or broad robustness guarantee. Crossover is SBX, mutation is bounded Gaussian. Added features do not always improve accuracy or communication.
- **Monte Carlo population:** 100 assumed random environments at 10 ms, not validated flight distributions. Numerical sensitivity changes channel/noise realizations between grids. No mission-reliability claim follows from success percentages.
- **Budget savings:** percentages use unlimited 10 Hz attempts as denominator. They do not by themselves prove improvement relative to a budget-constrained baseline. Effort is torque-squared integral, never joules.
- **Demo truthfulness:** 90 simulated seconds replay over 180 wall-clock seconds. Fault onsets are scheduled; detection, threshold changes, recovery and pass/fail are observed. The UI does not force nominal health or claim success at a scripted time.
